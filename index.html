The CWIP perspective

Design intellectual property from the endgame backward.

A patent is valuable only when it supports the future the company is trying to build.

CWIP begins with the corporate objective: financing, IPO, licensing, market entry or competitive defense. We then work backward to determine what the business must protect, what evidence must be built and how the portfolio should be designed.

Filing and prosecution are handled with trusted patent firms. CWIP remains focused on the higher-order question: whether the strategy will create leverage when the company is evaluated in the real world.

Strategic method

From foresight to defensible value.
